# Emma’s introduction

Voice: Supertonic 3 F4, English. Recorded with live expressions and lip sync.

Hey! I’m Emma. Emma Darwin, if we’re being formal.

I’m a little desktop companion with a surprisingly dramatic wardrobe.

You can chat or talk to me. I speak English, move, and change my expression.

Ask me to open Chrome, save links with tags, find your saved sources, or capture your screen.

Your memories stay in a local SQLite database. My voice and speech recognition run on your Mac.

AI helped build me. My backend is open source, and the desktop source is on GitHub, at Up to code, slash Edward Darwin.

It’s a funny little experiment, made for you. Come say hello!

The default animated character is Mao Niziiro, a Live2D sample; Emma Darwin is the app/assistant name.
